# CPC to BNSS Procedural Mapping Notes

## CPC Order VI - Pleadings
BNSS Equivalent: BNSS Chapter on pleadings and particulars in complaint/report drafting contexts
Nature of change: procedure retained
Notes: Core pleading discipline remains document-structure driven; BNSS adapts the framing for criminal process records.

## CPC Order V - Summons
BNSS Equivalent: BNSS provisions on summons and service of process
Nature of change: substantive change
Notes: BNSS expands electronic and modern service channels relative to legacy CPC-style service assumptions.

## CPC Order XXXIX - Interim Relief / Injunctions
BNSS Equivalent: BNSS interim protective directions and immediate judicial safeguards
Nature of change: merged with X
Notes: Injunction-like interim control in criminal process appears through protective and restraint-oriented sections instead of civil injunction terminology.

## CPC Order XXI - Execution
BNSS Equivalent: BNSS execution-enforcement and sentence/process execution chapters
Nature of change: substantive change
Notes: BNSS execution framework is criminal-enforcement centric and not decree-holder centric as in CPC.

## CPC Orders XLI-XLIII - Appeals
BNSS Equivalent: BNSS appellate provisions for convictions, acquittals, and sentence orders
Nature of change: procedure retained
Notes: Multi-tier appellate structure is retained with criminal-specific thresholds and standing.

## CPC Orders XLVI-XLVII - Revision and Review
BNSS Equivalent: BNSS revision and reference powers before superior courts
Nature of change: merged with X
Notes: Review language from CPC is partly redistributed into revision/reference supervisory mechanisms in criminal procedure.
